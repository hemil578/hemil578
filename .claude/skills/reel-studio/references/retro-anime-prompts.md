# Image prompts

Start every prompt with the style block, then add one shot type. Attach the approved character sheet to every
call. Fill in the square brackets. Keep characters original: invent the features and never name an existing
anime or film character.

## Style block

```
Retro Japanese TV anime from the late 1970s / early 1980s: flat cel colours with one hard shadow tone, slightly uneven brown-black ink outlines, muted faded film palette, soft grain like an old TV broadcast frame. NOT modern anime, NOT 3D. Original artwork, not copied from any existing anime or film. No text, no logos, no speech bubbles.
```

## Character sheet

```
Character reference sheet of one original character: [age, build, one exaggerated feature such as a huge jaw or thick brows, hair, outfit, personality]. Front and three-quarter views, plus three expressions: smug, worried, shocked. Plain background. Same face and clothes in every view.
```

## Character pose

One pose per image, never a grid.

```
Draw exactly the character from the attached sheet: same face, jaw, eyebrows, hair, skin tone and clothes. [Framing, e.g. head-and-chest shot, three-quarter view facing screen-left]. [Expression in physical detail]. [Light that matches the setting]. One character only. Transparent background (real alpha, no checkerboard). Portrait 4:5.
```

Expressions that work:

| Pose | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Lean | Leaning in, half-lidded eyes, confident smirk, one finger raised |
| Sweat | Eyes wide with tiny pupils, eyebrows raised in alarm, sweat drops on the forehead |
| Gulp | Same as sweat, lips pressed tight, dark shadow over the top of the face, red rim light |
| Grin with phone | Huge proud toothy grin, holding a modern black smartphone upright beside the face, screen facing straight at the viewer. The ENTIRE display is one solid flat pure green colour (#00FF00) |
| Smug | Eyes closed, chin up, satisfied grin |
| Shock | Jaw dropped wide open, eyes huge and white with tiny pupils, eyebrows shooting up, sweat drops flying, shock lines around the head |

## Open-mouth edit

Attach the pose itself, not the sheet.

```
Edit the attached image. Change only the mouth: open it as if mid-word (a relaxed "ah" shape), drawn in the same retro anime style. Keep everything else pixel-identical: pose, face, eyes, colours, outline, size, position, transparent background.
```

## Background

```
Background for a vertical 9:16 video, hand-painted watercolour-ish cels. [The setting, its light and three or four specific objects]. Foreground detail across the lower quarter of the frame. No people, no characters. Portrait 9:16.
```

## Tension backdrop

Used behind the gulp and shock shots.

```
Background for a vertical 9:16 video. An abstract dramatic suspense backdrop like a classic 70s anime tension shot: dark crimson and near-black with bold radiating speed lines from the centre, slight film grain. No people, no characters. Portrait 9:16.
```

## Extreme close-up on the eyes

```
Draw exactly this character's face, eyebrows and skin tone from the attached sheet. A dramatic extreme close-up filling a vertical 9:16 frame: only the upper half of the face, heavy eyebrows and narrowed intense half-lidded eyes staring straight at the viewer, a deep black shadow band across the top of the face, harsh red rim light, dark crimson background with radiating speed lines. Intense suspense. Portrait 9:16.
```

## Establishing shot (extended cut)

Drawn as a full frame, so the character belongs to the scene.

```
Vertical 9:16 full-frame shot. [Mood and light, e.g. low amber light, deep shadows]. [What the shot shows, with the character from the attached sheet if they appear in it]. Portrait 9:16.
```

Shot types that cut well in order: the place from outside, an arrival detail (shoes on a floor, a door), the
character in the room, the character at the key moment, a tight shot of the eyes.

## Hand holding the phone

Attach the teller's sheet so the sleeve and skin match.

```
Point-of-view shot: this character's hand holds a modern black smartphone up toward the viewer. The phone is perfectly upright and front-facing (no tilt, no perspective), with a thin black bezel, rounded corners and a small pill-shaped camera cutout at the top. The ENTIRE display area is one solid flat pure green colour (#00FF00, chroma key), with nothing drawn on it.
The hand grips the phone from below: the fingers wrap around the lower right edge and the back, the thumb rests on the lower left bezel; no finger or thumb covers the green display. The character's jacket sleeve comes up from the bottom edge of the image.
Composition: the phone fills most of the frame width and the top 78% of the image height; the hand and sleeve fill the lower part down to the bottom edge. Only the hand, sleeve and phone; transparent background (real alpha, no checkerboard). Portrait 9:16.
```
