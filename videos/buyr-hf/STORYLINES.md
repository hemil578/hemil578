# Buyr: storylines (pick one)

Fixed: 9:16, 24.6 s, cut to `track.mp3` (100 BPM, drop at 14.44 s), music and free sound effects, no voiceover. Real Buyr products, logo and site.

## A. "Saw it. Want it. Got it." (recommended)
*Device:* one object across apps. *Form:* object-to-object chain, with no hard cuts: each shot ends on the thing that opens the next.

The Glow Penguin glows in a vertical video and a thumb double-taps. On the opening hits the penguin **jumps out of the phone screen** into the real world and lands on the **real buyrshop.com product page**. A big cursor taps the real "Add to cart". The cart icon **morphs into the Buyr box**, which flies along a 3D route over a city to a front door. On the drop the door bursts open and every hit **fires a different product out of the box** across the screen, then they snap into the logo.

- First 2 s: the penguin glowing full-screen, with "SAW IT ON TIKTOK?" stamped on the 2nd hit.
- Beats: penguin in feed → pops out of the phone → real product page → tap Add to cart → cart becomes box → 3D map flight → drop: door opens, product burst on every beat → logo + buyrshop.com.
- Look: Buyr navy and teal, real photos and site, one material (soft studio light on cut-outs), with depth of field.
- Sound: the track, plus a pop, a tap click, a whoosh on the flight and a thud on each product.

## B. "WEIRD. CUTE. GENIUS."
*Device:* type carries the argument. *Form:* cut on the beat, with match cuts.

Giant poster type and flat colour fields change every half beat. Each word becomes a picture: "WEIRD" → old-man ski mask, "CUTE" → seal slippers, "GENIUS" → prism glasses. One circle shape holds through every cut (a frog's eye, a projector lens, a bowl) and finally becomes the arrow in the Buyr logo.

- First 2 s: "WEIRD" filling the screen on the first hit, its letters splitting open to reveal the ski mask.
- Beats: three words / three products → faster word-product pairs → drop: half-beat colour fields → "FREE SHIPPING" in giant type → circle match-cut into the logo.
- Look: Swiss poster style: huge condensed type, 5 flat brand colours, no gradients, no glow.
- Sound: the track, plus a hard snap on each type change.

## C. "The Endless Unboxing" (the bold one)
*Device:* the object is the explanation. *Form:* an endless zoom in 3D.

A 3D Buyr box spins open and the camera dives inside, into a little room built around one product. It dives into a detail of that product (the penguin's glow, the galaxy's stars), which opens into the next room, again and again, faster as the music builds. On the drop the box **explodes into an exploded view**: every product floats in 3D, labelled with name and price, orbiting the camera. Then it collapses back into the logo.

- First 2 s: the box lid blasting open toward the camera.
- Beats: box opens → dive → room 1 (penguin) → dive through glow → room 2 (galaxy) → … faster → drop: exploded orbit of all products → collapse to logo.
- Look: three.js studio, soft shadows, each room a different brand colour.
- Sound: the track, plus a whoosh on each dive and a boom on the explosion.
