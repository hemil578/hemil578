# Beat Sheet

## Summary
- Deliverable: Buyr "YOU WANT IT." reel, 24.6 s, 1080×1920 (9:16)
- Tone: deadpan → cute → satisfying → tense → hype
- Core story question: "Why would anyone buy this?" Answer: because you WANT it.

## Structure (measured beats)

| Time | Section | Footage | Text on screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.00–2.55 s | **HOOK** | 4 weirdest clips: abs projector, Santa butt, skull tongue, slime ball | Deadpan, one word per beat: YOU DON'T / NEED / THIS. |
| 2.55–4.95 s | **TURN** | Cutest clips: glow penguin, seal, cat can, frog | …YOU / WANT / IT. |
| 4.95–9.66 s | **SHOWCASE** | 4 products, 2 beats each | Name and price: Butter Squishy $19.99, Flower Pot $49.99, Dog Raincoat $26.99, Prism Glasses $24.99 |
| 9.66–12.45 s | **BREAKDOWN** | Gear cube, then keycap reaction. Black and white, slow motion, letterbox | WAIT. / what is that? |
| 12.45–14.26 s | **COUNTDOWN** | Digit display, clock pen, syringe pen | 3 / 2 / 1, one per beat. 0.12 s of silence before the drop |
| 14.26–21.43 s | **DROP** | 12 clips, rapid fire, full colour | HAS THEM ALL (3 beats) / FROM $9.99 (3 beats) / FREE US SHIPPING (3 beats) |
| 21.43–24.62 s | **OUTRO** | Last clip slows and blurs behind the teal card | YOU WANT IT. · buyrshop.com |

## Music And SFX Plan
- Sub hit on frame 1 (pattern interrupt).
- Whoosh into the turn.
- Tick on each price.
- Reverse swell into the breakdown.
- Tick on 3, 2 and 1, then a 0.12 s gap.
- Boom and white flash on the drop.
- Hit and shake on the 15.19 s kick and on each later kick.
- Final hit on the end card.
- Loudness target: −14 LUFS, true peak −1 dBTP.

## Shot Use Notes
- Every clip is used once, so no shot repeats.
- Clips shorter than their slot get slow motion; only the breakdown and outro are slowed.
- Grade by section:
  - Hook: deadpan, desaturated.
  - Turn: warm.
  - Showcase: clean.
  - Breakdown: black and white.
  - Drop: full colour pop.
  - Outro: teal.

## QC Plan
- Duration is 24.6 s at 1080×1920, 30 fps.
- Loudness −14 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP.
- Check every frame.
- Text stays on screen for at least 0.5 s + 0.3 s per word, inside the safe zone.
- No clip repeats.
