# "Every screen says yes": beat sheet

The `video` skill's Editor mode (role-modes.md §3) gives a timecoded beat sheet: what each shot does, its framing, its motion, and its sound. Track: Buyr `track.mp3`, 100 BPM, beats at 0.075 + 0.6k s, drop at 14.475 s.

| Time | Screen | Function | Framing / motion | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.000–0.675 | System warning dialog, c01 | Establish | Dialog punches in (scale 0.8→1), "YOU DON'T NEED THIS." | pop |
| 0.675–1.875 | Same dialog, c02 / c03 | Shift | Shake, line 1 struck through, "…YOU WANT IT." slams in | thud |
| 1.875–2.475 | Same dialog, c04 | Detail | Cursor travels and clicks BUY ANYWAY | tap |
| 2.475–4.275 | Shop grid | Reveal | White screen. Logo, "HAS THEM ALL", 3×3 tiles pop one row per beat | whoosh, taps |
| 4.275–9.075 | Scanner + receipt | Detail ×4 | A clip in the scanner each beat; a receipt line prints for each product | tap per line |
| 9.075–11.475 | Group chat | Reaction | WAIT— / WHAT— / LOL bubbles one per beat, then typing dots | pop per bubble |
| 11.475–12.675 | Mosaic | Pressure | The screen splits 1→4→9 tiles | tap |
| 12.675–14.475 | Countdown timer | Pressure | Ring drains one third per beat: 3, 2, 1. It holds still for a moment before the drop | tick ×3 |
| 14.475–16.875 | Shipping label | Impact | Flash, FREE US SHIPPING stamp slams, ON EVERY ORDER stamp, barcode scan | boom, thud ×2 |
| 16.875–18.075 | Price tag | Detail | Tag swings in on a string: FROM $9.99 | sparkle |
| 18.075–19.875 | Tracking map | Aftermath | Route draws, steps tick, TRACKED DELIVERY | whoosh, taps |
| 19.875–24.630 | Browser end card | Exit | URL types buyrshop.com, page builds, YOU WANT IT., cursor clicks SHOP NOW | taps, pop |

Every section is a different kind of screen, and no two sections share a transition style. Each text stays on screen for at least 0.5 s + 0.3 s per word.
