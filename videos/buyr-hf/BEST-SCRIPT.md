# Ad: Buyr — TikTok / Reels / Shorts · "YOU DON'T NEED THIS. …YOU WANT IT."

> One script, built with every ad skill in the repo:
> - `ads-creative-development` → performance structure (hook < 1 s, brand by 3 s, value by 5 s), TikTok norms (native look, cuts every 0.6–1.2 s, sound-off readable, real demos > polished)
> - `ads-video` → framework **Hook → Demo → CTA**, shot-by-shot table + storyboard
> - `ads-hooks` → 20 hooks over 5 angles (see `ADS-HOOKS.md`)
> - `shortform-hook-generator` → scored; winner **X2** (15/15)
> - `hyperframes-ad-director` → hook → problem → turn → proof → offer → CTA beat map, captions always, brand palette, no emoji
> - `motion-design` → "Energetic" personality, three motion layers, rising (vertical) motion language to match the music
> - `ads` / `ads-tiktok` creative specs → native, no fabricated UGC/testimonials/proof, sound-off safe, preview against TikTok UI overlays
> - `hyperframes` (build) + `ffmpeg-skill` (delivery check) come next, after you approve.

**Format:** 1080×1920, 30 fps, 24.6 s, music only (your track). All words are on-screen text, sized for phones (≥ 64 px), inside the safe band (y 168–1330, nothing under the right-hand action rail).
**Footage:** only the real demo videos from buyrshop.com product pages. Chinese captions and old text overlays are cropped out. **No product appears twice.** No licensed-character products (Goku/Luffy/Gengar). No crude products.
**Facts on screen (all from the site):** real prices; "Free US shipping on every order"; "Tracked delivery"; "Order early for the holidays" (honest reason: delivery takes 7–14 business days). No ratings, sales numbers or speed claims.

## The idea in one line
A **contrarian, honest hook** ("you don't need this… you want it") followed by **real products doing impossible-looking things on every beat**. The cuts **speed up as the music rises**, the drop delivers the offer, and the end card **bookends the hook** ("…you want it" → buyrshop.com).

## Motion system (`motion-design`)
- **Personality:** Energetic. Durations 100–250 ms; signature ease expo-out; overshoot 15–30% on text and stickers only (never on footage).
- **Entrance pattern (one, everywhere):** **rise from below.** Vertical motion = growth, matching the rising music.
- **Three layers:** primary = footage cuts on beats · secondary = text, price chips, logo · ambient = slow push-in on every clip (1.0→1.06) plus a 2-frame flash on the hits.
- **1/3 rule:** only one text element moves at a time; chips never move together with the headline.
- **Transitions (3 kinds only):** hard cut on hits · rising whip (sections and the build) · white flash (drop and logo).

## Sound
Your track at full level, plus small SFX on visible actions only: a squish (0.0), a pop (cat, 0.65), a click (keycap), a whoosh on the rising whips, a boom on the drop, a stamp on each offer line. Final mix −14 LUFS.

## Before posting: TikTok check (`ads` / `ads-tiktok` creative specs)
- Preview the export inside TikTok's upload screen to make sure no text sits under the UI.
- **Rights:** the product demo clips come from your product pages. Make sure you're allowed to use them in paid ads, especially clips showing people's faces (grandpa, ski mask, abs). The music track also needs a licence for ads. On TikTok, a commercial-library sound is safest.
- No fabricated UGC, reviews or results: this script uses none.
