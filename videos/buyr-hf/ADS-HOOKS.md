## Hook foundation
- **Business:** Buyr, a small US online shop of viral novelty gadgets ("the kind of things you see in a video and want to try yourself").
- **Audience:** US TikTok and Reels scrollers aged 16–35 who impulse-buy fun or "useless but cool" things, and holiday gift buyers.
- **Core pain:** boredom, and having no fun gift ideas.
- **Core desire:** to own the thing they saw in a video, and to give a gift that gets a reaction.
- **USP:** the viral finds in one shop, with free tracked US shipping on every order.
- **Proof available:** real demo footage, real reactions inside that footage (grandpa laughing, the ski-mask reveal), and real prices from $9.99.
- **Constraints (truth test):** delivery takes 7–14 business days, there are no returns, and there are no review counts or sales numbers on the site. Hooks must not imply fast delivery, ratings or sales figures.

## Recommendation
- **Best hook:** X2, text "YOU DON'T NEED THIS." on the first hit (0.05 s) over the stress-ball squeeze, then "…YOU WANT IT." on the second hit (0.65 s) as the cat pops up.
- **Reason:** it's specific, true, and contrarian. It sets up tension that every following clip pays off, and it uses both opening hits of the track.
- **A/B test:** the same video with X1 ("USELESS? MAYBE. FUN? ALWAYS.") and P2 ("OUT OF GIFT IDEAS?") as the opening text. Only the first 1.3 s changes.
- **Flags:** no review or sales-count claims; "order early" is only used with its real reason (7–14 business days).
