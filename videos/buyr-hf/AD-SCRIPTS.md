# Buyr: 3 ad scripts (pick one)

> Made with the ads-creative-development and ads-video skills (hooks written by hand; ads-hooks not used yet). Footage: the real product demo videos from buyrshop.com product pages (37 products have one).
> Format: TikTok / Reels / Shorts, 9:16, 24.6 s, **music only** (your track), on-screen text instead of voice, captions large and inside the safe zone.
> Track map: hits at 0.05 · 0.65 · 1.25 · 1.86 · 2.9 · 3.34 → calm build 3.3–7.8 → hits 7.85 · 8.45 · 9.06 → rising build 9.1–14.4 (steepest at 13.0) → **DROP 14.44** → full energy, peak 22.5 → end 24.6.
> Facts used (from the site only): real prices, "Free US shipping on every order", "tracked delivery". No delivery-speed claims, and no claims about returns.

## Script 1: "TikTok made me buy it" (Hook → Demo → CTA) · **recommended**

**Idea:** a fast, satisfying montage of products *doing their thing*, cut to every hit. Cuts get faster as the music rises; the drop becomes a burst of the best payoffs. It's the native "#tiktokmademebuyit" format people already watch.

|---|---|---|---|
| 0.00–0.65 | hit | **Stress-ball face squeezed, bubbles out** (pattern interrupt, frame 0 = thumbnail) | **TIKTOK MADE ME BUY IT** |
| 0.65–1.25 | hit | Cat hook: keys hung → **cat pops up** | |
| 1.25–1.86 | hit | Tomato slicer slicing through | |
| 1.86–2.90 | hit | Penguin goes dark → **glows** | |
| 2.90–3.34 | hit | Ice-ball cup: cola pours over the ice ball | **…SO I DID** |
| 3.3–7.8 | calm build | 4 demos × ~1.1 s (butter squishy slowly rising, flowers lighting up, dog in raincoat walking, prism glasses reading in bed). Each gets a small name + price sticker | e.g. *Butter Squishy · $19.99* |
| 7.85 / 8.45 / 9.06 | 3 hits | Three punch-in moments: grandpa laughing at the **Santa tissue holder**, the **old-man ski mask** reveal, the **abs projector** on a body | **WAIT** · **WHAT** · **😂** (text, no emoji font) |
| 9.1–14.4 | rising | One demo per beat, then per half-beat from 13 s: gear cube, keycap clicker, berry bowl rinsing, egg poacher, magnetic frog, pop-up skull keychain, clock pen, light-up keyboard… | price stickers only |
| **14.44** | **DROP** | White flash → rapid "best moments" recap at 2 cuts per beat, each with a bold colour frame | **FREE US SHIPPING** → **ON EVERY ORDER** → **TRACKED TO YOUR DOOR** |
| 19.9–24.6 | peak | Logo slams in over a split-grid of 4 products moving; **buyrshop.com** button pulses on the beat | **TRENDING FINDS. SHIPPED FREE.** · buyrshop.com |

**Why it should work:** the hook is a real "what is that?!" visual in the first half-second. Every cut lands on a beat, so it feels edited *to* the song. Speed rises with the build, and the drop is the payoff.

## Script 2: "Gifts for people who have everything" (Problem → Solution → Proof)

**Idea:** holiday gifting. It's November, and Buyr's catalogue is a gift list. The problem is relatable, the products are the funny solution, and the proof is real people laughing in the footage.

| Time | Music | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.00–3.34 | hits | Quick text slams on bold colour fields, one word per hit, ending on the **old-man ski mask** reveal | **WHAT DO YOU GET** · **THE PERSON** · **WHO HAS** · **EVERYTHING?** |
| 3.3–7.8 | calm build | "Gift ideas" as a list: 4 products, each in its demo moment, with a gift tag "for ___" | *for grandpa* (Santa tissue) · *for the dog lover* (raincoat) · *for the night owl* (glow penguin) · *for the lazy one* (prism glasses) |
| 7.85 / 8.45 / 9.06 | 3 hits | Three real reactions: grandpa laughing, ski-mask guy, abs projector | **HE LOST IT** · **THEY LOST IT** · **YOU WILL TOO** |
| 9.1–14.4 | rising | More "for ___" tags, one per beat, getting faster | *for your boss* · *for the cook* · *for the gamer* · … |
| **14.44** | **DROP** | Flash → price stickers slamming onto products, 2 per beat | **MOST UNDER $30** → **FREE US SHIPPING** → **TRACKED** |
| 19.9–24.6 | peak | Logo, gift-box end card | **ORDER EARLY FOR THE HOLIDAYS** · buyrshop.com |

**Why it should work:** it speaks to an urgent seasonal need (gifts), and the proof comes from real reactions in your footage. "Order early" is honest: delivery takes 7–14 business days. *Note: "most under $30" is true. 43 of your 50 products are under $30, checked against the store.*

## Script 3: "POV: you finally bought everything TikTok showed you" (UGC-style POV)

**Idea:** first-person POV, the most native TikTok format. Almost all your footage is already hands-on POV, so it reads like a real person showing off their haul.

| Time | Music | Shot | On-screen text (TikTok-style caption font) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.00–3.34 | hits | Caption bubble first, then hands-on demos cut on each hit | **POV: you finally bought everything TikTok showed you** |
| 3.3–7.8 | calm build | "Day 1": slow, satisfying demos (butter rising, berry bowl, egg poacher) | *day 1: obsessed* |
| 7.85–9.1 | 3 hits | "Day 2": the funny ones (ski mask, abs projector, Santa) | *day 2: my family is concerned* |
| 9.1–14.4 | rising | "Day 3": faster and faster through the rest | *day 3: I need more* |
| **14.44** | **DROP** | Burst montage, 2 cuts per beat | **NO REGRETS** → **FREE US SHIPPING** |
| 19.9–24.6 | peak | Logo end card | **your turn →** buyrshop.com |

**Why it should work:** POV captions look like organic content, so viewers drop their "ad shield". It's funny, relatable, and drives comments.
